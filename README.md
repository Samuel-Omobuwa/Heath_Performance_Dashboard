# Healthcare Performance & Patient Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive **Healthcare Performance & Patient Analytics Dashboard** built with **Microsoft Power BI** to provide a consolidated view of patient activity, operational performance, and financial performance.

The dashboard transforms healthcare data into actionable insights, enabling stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators, understand patient patterns, evaluate financial performance, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project was to develop an interactive business intelligence solution that helps healthcare management:

* Monitor patient volume and healthcare service utilization
* Understand patient demographics and diagnosis patterns
* Identify geographical differences in patient activity
* Track revenue, cost, and profitability
* Monitor operational indicators such as waiting time and patient satisfaction
* Provide an interactive platform for exploring performance across different months, states, genders, and departments

---

## 💡 Key Business Questions

### Patient & Operational Analysis

* How many patients are being served?
* How many visits are recorded?
* Which states have the highest patient volumes?
* Which age groups represent the largest patient population?
* What are the most common diagnoses?
* How are patients distributed by gender and location?
* How does patient activity change over time?
* How does patient activity vary across departments?

### Financial Analysis

* What is the total revenue generated?
* What is the total operational cost?
* What is the total profit?
* What is the average revenue generated per patient?
* Which states contribute the most revenue?
* How does revenue change over time?
* Where are the major opportunities for improving financial performance?

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The dashboard is organized into three major analytical areas.

### Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of overall healthcare performance.

Key metrics include:

* Total Patients
* Total Visits
* Total Revenue
* Total Cost
* Total Profit
* Average Revenue per Patient
* Average Waiting Time
* Average Satisfaction

The page also provides visual analysis of visit trends and revenue by state.

### Patient Analysis

Provides deeper insight into the patient population and healthcare utilization.

Key analysis includes:

* Patients by State
* Patient Age Groups
* Gender Distribution
* Diagnosis Analysis
* Patient Visit Volume
* Department Filtering
* Monthly Analysis

The dashboard highlights **Rivers** as the top state by displayed patient volume, **Malaria** as the top diagnosis, and **31–45** as the largest displayed age group.

### Financial Performance

Focuses on the financial health of the healthcare operation.

Key metrics include:

* Revenue
* Cost
* Profit
* Average Revenue per Patient
* Revenue by State
* Revenue Trends

This view enables stakeholders to evaluate financial performance alongside operational activity.

---

## 🔍 Key Dashboard Insights

Based on the dashboard:

* **Rivers** recorded the highest displayed patient volume with **322 patients**.
* **Malaria** was the most common displayed diagnosis with **283 patients**.
* The **31–45 age group** was the largest displayed age segment with **361 patients**.
* Total displayed revenue was approximately **₦24.44M**.
* Total displayed cost was approximately **₦15.01M**.
* Total displayed profit was approximately **₦9.43M**.
* Average revenue per patient was approximately **₦40.19K**.
* Average waiting time was **45.42**.
* Average patient satisfaction was **3.72**.

> KPI values are based on the dashboard view shown and may change when filters are applied.
